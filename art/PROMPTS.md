# Image generation inputs

Mode: built-in imagegen, `transparent_background=true`, three new atlas images. The original PNGs are retained beside this file. Technical import, palette assignment, exact UV maps and JSON geometry are reproducible with `tools/generate_assets.py`.

## weapons.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production sprite atlas for a Minecraft 1.21.1 historical weapons mod, to be sliced into game inventory icons. Generate a single precisely organized 6-column by 3-row atlas, landscape 2:1 aspect. Every cell identical square size, no grid lines, no labels, absolutely transparent empty background. One separate weapon in each of 18 cells. Each weapon has generous 12 percent transparent margin and must never touch another cell. All metal neutral silver steel with crisp pixel art shading; handles dark brown leather / wood with small brass pins. Minecraft-compatible detailed pixel art, clean hard pixel edges, each icon reads as a 64x64 sprite. All weapons diagonal from bottom-left handle to upper-right tip and entirely within their own square. Exact row-major order: row 1 arming sword, two handed European longsword, flanged mace, medieval spear, curved Japanese katana, short medieval dagger. Row 2 fixed-head spiked morningstar (NOT chain), bearded battle axe, halberd, compact war hammer with rear pick, slender rapier with basket hilt, curved sabre. Row 3 Roman gladius, Nepalese kukri with inward curved broad blade, Indian katar push dagger with H-shaped grip, Japanese naginata, long pike with small steel tip, chain flail with ONE spiked ball and visible short chain. Different recognizable silhouette for every weapon. No text, no shadows on background, no scenery, no decorative border, no colored background, no checkerboard painted into the image. This is an exact 6x3 sprite sheet game asset.

## Armor surface sheet — 1.0.1

Built-in image generation; project source: `art/armor_surfaces.png`.
The sheet supplies eight albedo materials. `tools/generate_armor.py` unwraps the
actual game geometry into separate UV islands, converts metal palettes, adds
visor vents and edge highlights, and writes the 80 final 1024×1024 textures.
The original inventory icons stay unchanged and are the silhouette reference.

Prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production Minecraft armor material surface texture sheet to be UV-mapped onto modeled medieval knight armor. Create one square image completely filled by a precise 4 column by 2 row grid of EIGHT equally sized square material surface swatches, NO gaps, NO labels, NO borders between cells, NO objects or armor silhouettes, NO perspective. All eight patches are flat orthographic tileable material diffuse/albedo surfaces in refined hand-painted Minecraft pixel art with clean readable pixel clusters, fairly subtle values, no photographic noise or strong cast shadows. Top row left to right: (1) polished cool silver forged plate steel, softly brushed surface with restrained fine hammer marks, (2) steel riveted chainmail rings, clear dense interlocking rings on charcoal backing, (3) overlapping small rectangular steel lamellar scales tied with tiny ochre laces, (4) rich oxblood burgundy woven brigandine cloth studded by a regular spaced pattern of small silver rivets. Bottom row: (1) muted warm brass etched trim surface, (2) rich dark brown leather with subtle stitched grain, (3) ivory quilted linen gambeson padding with small diamond stitch grid, (4) deep blue charcoal woven padded lining. Material colors should match polished knight armor, warm brass trim and dark brown leather of high quality detailed Minecraft inventory icons. The steel and mail and lamellar MUST be neutral silver grayscale except tiny laces, for subsequent palette swapping. Use a 1024 square composition, keep each swatch exactly square with straight grid edges at quarters and halfway, no text, no watermark.


## 1.0.2 — квадратная броня Minecraft

Текущий лист иконок: `art/armor_square.png`. Создан встроенной генерацией изображений. Производственный импорт выполняет `tools/generate_armor.py`: 32×32, nearest-neighbour, порог прозрачности и палитра по материалу. Надетые модели генерируются отдельно из кубоидов; их UV-карты рисуются точно по развёртке плоскими пиксельными оттенками. Старые округлые источники оставлены только как история проекта.

Финальный промпт:

Brand new game pixel-art sprite sheet for a Minecraft mod. This is NOT a realistic medieval armor painting. Draw strictly vanilla Minecraft inventory item icons as a FOUR BY FOUR grid of sixteen sprites on a fully TRANSPARENT background. Every sprite has a logical 16 by 16 pixel grid and is enlarged only by integer nearest-neighbour scaling; very large visible square pixels, a tiny 16-color palette, no gradients and no antialiasing. Every helmet is a PERFECT RECTANGULAR CUBE with a long FLAT HORIZONTAL TOP EDGE and straight vertical sides: no dome, no cone, no diagonals, no curves, no chamfered corners, no stepped round shape. Armor pieces consist exclusively of straight rectangular panels and rectangular shoulder pads. Boots are pairs of straight rectangular boxes with horizontal square flat toes. Column order: helmet, chestplate, leggings, pair of boots. Row order: 1 chainmail with cream trim and dark checker ring pixels; 2 rectangular steel lamellar plates with ochre fastening pixels; 3 oxblood-red riveted brigandine with grey rectangular open helmet and rectangular horizontal brim; 4 iron knight plate armor, rectangular CLOSED CUBE HELMET with square protruding visor, black horizontal eye slit and a few black square vent pixels. In every row the chest icon is a flat rectangular torso with two blocky rectangular shoulders, leggings are two upright straight rectangular legs joined by a square belt, boots are two little upright rectangular boots with 90-degree toes. Use only chunky rectangles of grey iron, dark outlines, brown leather, cream and small gold fitting pixels; use burgundy only for brigandine. Flat front or slight block-side view, crisp hard flat color regions. Leave at least 2 logical pixels transparent padding inside each equally sized cell. The primary requirement is square block-shaped silhouettes like vanilla Minecraft's iron armor icons. No circular shapes anywhere, no polished-metal reflections, no realistic light, no text or labels, no shadows cast on background.


## Щиты 1.0.3

Десять текущих форм, все текстуры и иконки щитов создаются точным пиксельным генератором `tools/generate_shields.py`. Граница модели, граница иконки и лицевые UV-области используют одну и ту же маску. Генератор экспортирует только внешние поверхности, цельную металлическую окантовку, умбон и заднюю рукоять. Все руки привязаны к рукояти, без смещения относительно растрового изображения.

Старая иллюстрация `art/shields.png` сохранена как история проекта; в ресурсах щитов 1.0.3 она не используется.


## Оружие 1.0.4

Все текущие 18 форм оружия и 180 UV-текстур создаются точным генератором `tools/generate_weapons.py` на сетке 0,5 единицы Minecraft. Внешние грани объединённых тел экспортируются в обычные модели Minecraft JSON. Для каждого материала сохраняются отдельные точные UV-атласы. Модели в инвентаре и руках используют одну геометрию. Дополнительные PNG-иконки 64×64 — технические проекции этой же геометрии.

Исходная иллюстрация `art/weapons.png` сохранена как история проекта; модели и текстуры оружия 1.0.4 из неё не вырезаются. `art/weapon_catalog.png` обновляется снимком настоящего игрового каталога после проверки.
